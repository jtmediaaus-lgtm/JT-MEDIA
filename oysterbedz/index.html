Welcome to Oysterbedz

Dolphin Quay's only small bar

Tucked into The Palladio on the Mandurah Ocean Marina, Oysterbedz is the kind of place locals keep to themselves. Relaxed, intimate and right on the water — we pour speciality drinks and serve the freshest seafood with nothing between you and the marina but a sea breeze.

Our oysters arrive fresh and are shucked to order, finished a dozen different ways — from clean and natural to a proper Kilpatrick. Add a tasting platter, a basket of golden chips with rosemary sea salt, and you've found your new favourite spot.

  • Fresh, locally-sourced oysters & seafood
  • Outdoor seating with uninterrupted marina views
  • Speciality cocktails, wine & cold beer
  • Walk-ins welcome — bookings recommended
